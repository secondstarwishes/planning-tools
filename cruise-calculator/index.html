💡 Planning Tips

Final Payment: Must be paid in full by 11:59 PM EST to avoid cancellation.
Online Check-in: Complete as soon as it opens to select your Port Arrival Time and upload required documents.
Booking Window: Book shore excursions, spa treatments, and adult dining at your booking window for the best availability.
Travel Insurance: Consider adding travel insurance before your final payment date for maximum coverage.
Passport Validity: Ensure passports are valid for at least 6 months after your return date (if required).
